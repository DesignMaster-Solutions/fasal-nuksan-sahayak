                                       ORDER
Order No.                             14.10.2022

 05.         1.      Mr. Muduli, learned advocate appears on behalf of
             petitioner and submits, his client suffered paddy crop loss due
             to cyclonic rainfall between 14th and 15th November, 2021. He
             submits, on 16th November, 2021 his client gave intimation to
             the bank, regarding the loss. The insurance company (opposite
             party no.2) has filed counter repudiating the claim on
             allegation that intimation was not given within 72 hours of the
             calamity.

             2.      Mr. Mishra, learned senior advocate appears on behalf
             of opposite party no.2 and draws attention to disclosure at
             annexure-4 bearing date 24th November, 2021 to submit, this
             was first intimation of loss given to his client, much after 72
             hours of the weather activity. On query from Court Mr.
             Mishra submits, petitioner was insured through the bank. The
             bank did not intimate prior to 24th November, 2021. Mr. Dash,
                                                                  Page 1 of 2
       learned advocate appears on behalf of the bank (opposite party
      no.3). He prays for leave to file additional affidavit to deal
      with the allegation.

      3.    The bank has leave to file additional affidavit for
      disclosing acknowledgement of receipt of intimation given by
      it to the insurance company and the policy. The affidavit will
      be accepted on adjourned date upon advance copy served to
      petitioner and the insurance company.

      4.    List on 20th October, 2022.
