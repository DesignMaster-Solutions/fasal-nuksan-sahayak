14.      We have carefully gone through the order of the State
Commission, the District Commission, the other relevant records, and
the rival contentions of the parties. As has been held by Hon'ble
Supreme Court in catena of judgments1 the revisional jurisdiction of the
National Commission is extremely limited, it should be exercised only in
case as contemplated within the parameters specified in the provision
i.e. when State Commission had exercised a jurisdiction not vested in it
by law or had failed to exercise jurisdiction so vested or had acted in
the exercise of its jurisdiction so vested illegally or with material
irregularity. It is only when such findings are found to be against any
provisions of law or against the pleadings or evidence or are found to
be wholly perverse, a case for interference may call for at the second
appellate (revisional) jurisdiction. In the present case, there are
concurrent findings of both the Forums below, under which only the
insurance company has been held liable for deficiency in service, and
no liability has been fixed on the bank.

 15.    We have also carefully gone through the operational guidelines of
the   PMFBY.       The    scheme   contains   details   of the   roles   and
responsibilities of all the stakeholders. In particular, the bank and the
insurance company have well-defined roles. While there is no doubt
that in cases of substantial misreporting or furnishing of wrong details of
insured farmers by the bank, the concerned banks has to be held liable,
such allegations of substantial misreporting/wrongful furnishing of
details by the bank have to be established by the insurance company.


16.    In the present case, the main contention of the insurance
company rests on the wrongful submission of details of the farmers by
the Bank.      Hence, the insurance company was given sufficient
opportunities to place on record the requisite details and documents
pertaining to the cases, notwithstanding that in normal situations,
additional documents/evidence should generally not be permitted at
revision stage. However, keeping in view the Larger Bench judgment of
this Commission in RP/533/2019, which placed reliance on the

hold that in exceptional cases, this Commission is well within its powers
to allow additional documents/evidence to be filed at the stage of the
revision petition.

been filed by the insurance company in compliance with this
Commission's orders on different dates as stated in the preceding
paragraphs. However, considering that the bank has challenged the
veracity/correctness of the details filed by the insurance company vide
Annexure A-1 to the affidavit dated 05.08.2026, and these details are

18.   Other than the above-stated details, which are not found reliable,

establish beyond reasonable doubt that the bank had furnished wrong
details of the farmers in question.


19.    It is to be noted that even in the details furnished earlier by the

dismissed on account of non-reporting and localized claims. It was
submitted during the hearing that the scheme envisages reporting in
case of localized claims within 72 hours, but the farmers failed to report
such claims within the prescribed timeline. However, we are unable to
accept such arguments, as once such incidents have happened in
