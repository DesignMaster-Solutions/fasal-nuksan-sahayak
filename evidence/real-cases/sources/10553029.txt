                       ORAL ORDER
 BY THE HON'BLE MRS. SUNITA CHANNABASAPPA BAGEWADI,
 LADY MEMBER:-

      This appeal is filed by the Appellant/Opposite Party No.2
  being aggrieved by the Order dated 24.02.2023 passed by the
  Bangalore I Additional District Consumer Disputes Redressal
                                    2
                                                    SC/29/A/1168/2023


Commission, Bangalore in CC.No.03/2022 (Rural) and prays to
set-aside the order passed by the District Commission by
allowing appeal, in the interest of justice and equity.

   2. The brief facts of the case are as under:-

   The complainant is the owner of lands bearing Sy.Nos.69/2,
69/3 and 70/6 totally measuring 2 acres 8 guntas at
Hadonahalli Village, Doddaballapura Taluk. The complainant
grown Ragi crop in the said land and insured the crop with
Opposite Party No.2/Insurance Company under the crop
insurance. Due to continuous rains for a period of 20 days his
entire Ragi crop has been damaged.          Hence, he met the
agricultural officer and intimated about the loss caused to the
Ragi crop due to heavy rain.       The agricultural officer and
Assistant Agricultural Director of Doddabalapura visited his
land and inspected the same and prepared loss report and
submitted the same on 09.12.2020 to Assistant Director of
Agriculture.    After 10 days the complainant visited the
Assistant Director of Agriculture, who told that after 3 months
the compensation will be paid to him. After 3 months when he
visited the Assistant Agricultural officer, who told him to meet
agricultural officer at Tubagere. Accordingly, he met the said
officer who informed the complainant that the insurance
money was not sanctioned. Hence, on 16.11.2021 he met the
Joint Director of Agriculture, Bengaluru and filed complaint,
who informed him to visit after one week, but there was no
response from the agricultural department.         Therefore, on
29.12.2021 he sent a letter to insurance company requesting
to pay Rs.33,801/-, the loss assessed by the Agricultural
                                            3
                                                              SC/29/A/1168/2023


Officer and Assistant Agricultural Director of Doddabalapura.
But the insurance company not replied to the said letter.
There after he met the Assistant Agricultural Director who
again informed to the complainant to meet the insurance agent
at Doddablapura.          When he contacted the insurance agent
through mobile, he informed that since crop loss report has
not been received within two months, he is not entitled for the
insurance amount. Hence, this complaint.

   3.    After service of notice of the District Commission, the
Opposite Parties appeared through its counsel. The Opposite
Party No.1 not filed version.         The Opposite Party No.2 filed
version and admitted that the Opposite Party No.2 has issued
crop insurance policy under Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima
Yojana, covering the crop season of Kharif 2020 for the
cultivation of crop Fingermillet (Ragi/Mandika) covering losses
due     to   perils/calamities     i.e.,       landslide,   Hailstorm   and
inundation, cloud burst and natural fire etc.                       Further
contended that appointment of loss assessors shall be made
within 48 hours from the date of damage of crops. However, in
the instant case, after lapse of 8 months loss assessor was
appointed. Hence, there is no deficiency in service on the part
of the opposite parties.
   4.    After   trial,   the   District       Commission     allowed   the
complaint in part.

  5. Aggrieved by the said order the Appellant/Opposite Party
No.2 preferred this Appeal.

  6. Heard from both parties.
                                   4
                                                  SC/29/A/1168/2023


  7. Perused the memorandum of appeal and certified copy of
the order passed by the District Consumer Commission and
documents on record, we noticed that it is not in dispute that
the Respondent No.1 is the owner of the lands bearing
Sy.Nos.69/2, 69/3 and 70/6 totally measuring 2 acres 8
guntas at Hadonahalli Village, Doddaballapura Taluk.         It is
also not in dispute that in 2020 the Respondent No.1 grown
Ragi crop in the said land and insured with the Opposite Party
No.2 under Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana. The allegation
of the Respondent No.1 is that due to heavy rain for a period of
20 days, Ragi crop grown in his lands were totally damaged
and sustained loss of Rs.33,801/-. However, the Appellant has
not settled the claim of the Respondent No.1 even though
Assistant Agricultural Director has submitted the loss report
after investigation.   Per-Contra, Appellant contended that
regarding crop loss no intimation has been received in time in
Kharif 2020 season.    But after 7 months of loss, intimation
was sent by Agricultural Department on 26.07.2021 and as per
the guidelines it is not eligible.    The loss ought to have
intimated within 72 hours by the insured farmer and it's
contain all details i.e. the name of the farmer, survey number
of lands, crop grown etc and bank shall provide payment
verification within 48 hours and appointment of loss assessor
shall be made within 48 hours.          To that effect mobile
application may be used for reporting the insurance claim.

   8. Perused Ex.P1 crop loss report submitted by Agriculture
Officer, Tubagere, along with Assistant Agricultural Director of
Doddabalapura stating that "Due to heavy rain since 2 weeks
the Ragi crop grown by the Respondent No.1 was damaged".
                                      5
                                                       SC/29/A/1168/2023


The said report prepared by the Agricultural Department is an
official document prepared by competent authority. As such it
is sufficient to settle the claim of the Respondent No.1.
However, the Appellant repudiated the claim on technical
grounds such as the Respondent No.1 has not intimated the
Appellant within 72 hours after loss of crops and hence fails to
appoint the loss assessor.        If there is report of competent
authority on record, the Appellant ought to settle the claim of
the Respondent No.1. The Respondent No.1 insured his crops
under Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana and the scheme is
introduced for farmers and object is to provide financial
protection to farmers against crop loss caused by natural
calamities, pests and diseases to stabilize farmer's income.
Moreover, farmers are the back bone of our country. Economy
and despite facing challenges such as natural calamities, pests
and fluctuating market prices, they continue to work.          Their
contribution is vital for our country's economic growth. Hence,
considering the facts and discussion made here, we are of the
opinion that the repudiation of the claim by the Appellant on
technical grounds is not just and proper. As such, the order
passed by the District Commission is just and proper.               No
interference is required. Accordingly, we proceed to pass the
following:-
                              ORDER

           The appeal is dismissed. No order as to costs.
           The impugned order dated 24.02.2023 passed by
     the      Bangalore   I    Additional   District   Consumer
     Disputes      Redressal     Commission,     Bangalore     in
     CC.No.03/2022 (Rural) is confirmed.
